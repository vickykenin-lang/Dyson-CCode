# Voice benchmark, 19 Oct 2026 (D-024, LA-APP-08)

Constraints from the Founder: Sydney only, the AWS free plan (no upgrade), and a central process
(audio is heard on Dyson's side, not on the device). Test audio: Amazon Polly Kajal (neural, hi-IN
and en-IN) speaking four synthetic phrases, so these numbers are a first check, not the threshold.
The Founder's own recordings set the thresholds (LA-PERF-02).

| Option | Result |
|---|---|
| Amazon Transcribe streaming (Sydney) | Refused: the free plan's service control policy denies `transcribe:StartStreamTranscription` |
| Amazon Nova 2 Sonic (live speech model, Hindi) | Not offered in Sydney (US, Tokyo, Stockholm) |
| GPT Realtime, Gemini Live | Not tested: audio would leave AWS and Sydney |
| On-device browser recognition | Not used: the Founder wants the process central |
| Whisper tiny / base / small on the server (int8, 2 threads) | Hinglish wrong or garbled; small took 10 to 12 s for 2 to 4 s of audio |
| **AI4Bharat IndicConformer (hi), int8, on the server** | **Chosen.** About 1.2 s for 4 s of audio on 1 to 1.5 CPU |

IndicConformer on the server (voice container, no network):

| Said | Heard | Marked | Took |
|---|---|---|---|
| टी ज़ीरो पर सात और आठ जोड़ दो | टी सीरो पर सात और आठ जोड़ दो | unsure | 0.9 s |
| T0 par 7 aur 8 jod do, aur batao aaj kya pending hai | टी ज़ीरो पार् सात और आठ जोर दो और बताओ आज क्या पेंडिंग है | unsure | 1.2 s |
| Mujhe aaj ke saare approvals dikhao aur jo urgent hai pehle batao | मुझे आज के सारेप्रूवल्स दिखाओ और जो अर्जेंट है पहले बताओ | sure | 1.2 s |
| What is pending for me today? | व्ॉट इज स्पेंडिंग फॉर मी टु डे | unsure | 0.6 s |

Every misheard phrase was marked unsure, so it goes to the Founder to confirm (LA-APP-07). English
comes back in Devanagari. Starting thresholds: every token at least 0.60 likely and a mean of at
least 0.85; to be set from the Founder's recordings.
