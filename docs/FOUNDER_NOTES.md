## 17 Oct 2026: Survival mode when the Founder does not respond (AMD-12, gate 7)

In the Founder's words: if the Founder does not respond for 3 days, Dyson runs in survival mode.
The system does not shut down. It keeps recovering, keeps managing the Receptors, keeps doing
maintenance, and can contact the Founder's nominee.

To settle before it can be built (it amends AMD-12 v1.0):

- AMD-12 today says 48 hours; this note says 3 days (72 hours).
- AMD-12 says no one else inherits Founder authority. Contacting the nominee must stay
  notification only, unless the Founder decides the nominee may decide something.
- Who the nominee is and how they are reached (kept as personal data under D-017).
- How "no response" is detected and verified (gate 7's open sub-point).
- What survival mode may do: AMD-12 limits it to previously approved low-risk work and halts new
  high-risk or financial decisions.

**18 Oct 2026:** the Founder named the nominee. The name, email and WhatsApp number are kept only in
the encrypted parameter `/dyson/nominee` (never in this repository, never sent to a model). The
amendment "Amend AMD-12" (72 hours, Survival Mode, nominee notified only, presence = app login,
message or Direct Founder Controls action) was proposed to Direct Founder Controls; it expires
unanswered on 19 Oct 2026 at 16:55 UTC.
